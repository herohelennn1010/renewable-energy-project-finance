## Comparability and extraction policy

Investment properties changed from cost to fair-value measurement in 2025 [S01, note A2]. Use restated FY2024 profit and balance-sheet figures. Original FY2024 figures appear only in the explicit restatement bridge. FY2023 P&L is not retrospectively re-presented in the latest five-year table; retain it as originally reported with a comparison warning. Its main balance-sheet series also uses the original cost-model snapshot from S02. Opening 2024 restated assets/equity are separately disclosed, not silently substituted into FY2023 ratios.

Total operating, investing and financing cash flows are unaffected by the accounting change [S01, note A2]. Cash-flow scope may include discontinued operations, while revenue is continuing operations. Management EBITDA excludes certain items and is not a simple addition of cash-flow-statement D&A to PBT. Keep it as a sourced non-GAAP input; the analysis also calculates a transparent P&L coverage proxy without inventing an EBITDA reconciliation.

## Market evidence and source conflicts

EMA capital-cost guidance [S14] contextualises the hypothetical EPC price, but is not a current project quote. Irradiance [S15] and electrical specific yield have different units; no bankable yield study is implied.

EMC's 2025 USEP [S16] is an annual, all-hours wholesale average. Carrying it into future years, applying a solar capture haircut and choosing a corporate PPA price are separate assumptions. No retail tariff is used as an export tariff. Dated policy sources [S13] are distinguished from live pages without publication dates, which are labelled observations at cutoff.

Conflicts are quarantined: S05 shows 1H2025 investing cash flow of S$114m, whereas S03/S04 show S$175m. Statutory statements prevail, and management FCF is excluded from the core cash-surplus proxy. S24 search and direct-page capacity figures differ, so its quantitative capacity is excluded. Primary S23 gas-disruption figures prevail over conflicting secondary headlines, without asserting a quantified Sembcorp loss.
