# Phase 3 — Assumptions and financing structure

**All project-specific amounts and terms are hypothetical.** Public sponsor data and market observations are stored separately. This brief is ready for modelling; it is not a loan recommendation.

Commitment / budget cost is **65.36%**, below the illustrative 70% structural ceiling. This is **debt-to-cost, not LTV**, and does not demonstrate cash-flow debt capacity. No independent asset valuation or refinancing proceeds are assumed.

Inject permanent equity into a controlled account at close and spend it before senior drawings. Allocate EPC/contingency across six construction quarters at 10%, 20%, 25%, 25%, 15% and 5%, evenly within each quarter. Development and upfront fee are paid at close; initial liquidity and reserve funding at COD. Actual interest and fees are calculated from outstanding and undrawn balances. Do not spend the S$5m IDC envelope as if it were an interest output or add it again after calculating interest.

Unused budget allocations remain restricted pending completion tests. Actual debt need can be below commitment; do not draw solely to exhaust the facility. Reallocation within the S$153m envelope must be disclosed. Above-budget costs use the separate capped support facility or remain a funding shortfall.

Refundable GST is outside permanent costs and is timed separately. A subordinated sponsor bridge is capped at S$15m; refunds repay that bridge. Neither bridge drawings nor repayments are CFADS. Phase 4 must calculate utilisation and flag cap breaches.

## Operating assumptions

Specific electrical yield: 1,250 kWh/kWp/year after conversion and ordinary shading losses, at 100% availability and zero external curtailment. Apply 98% availability and 1% curtailment once, then 0.5% annual degradation after year one. Allocate by calendar days as a simplification; no certified P50/P90 or seasonal resource profile is claimed.

Sell 80% of generation at S$180/MWh under 20-year fixed nominal PPAs. Merchant revenue uses 20% of production × S$116.57/MWh historical reference × 85% capture factor. A separate S$3/MWh fee applies to merchant production. After PPA expiry, all generation becomes merchant. These prices and arrangements are assumptions, not disclosed Sembcorp contracts or forward-market quotations.

Initial recurring costs: O&M S$12/kWp/year; roof access S$6/kWp/year; insurance 0.3% of original EPC; administration S$0.2m/year. Escalate at 2% annually. Replacement capex at the end of operating year 12 equals 5% of original EPC, escalated. Decommissioning at the end of year 25 equals S$2m in COD-price terms, escalated; salvage value is zero. Receivables and recurring opex payables use 30 days; capex, taxes and financing are excluded from opex payables.

Book depreciation and tax allowances are separate. Assume original EPC and replacement equipment qualify for a three-year tax write-off; no other cost qualifies without verification. Assume loss continuity conditions and interest deductibility are met, and no cash tax refunds arise from losses. The quarterly tax accrual/one-quarter cash lag is a modelling simplification, not a statement of Singapore payment rules.

## Debt mechanics

Senior commitment S$100m; availability 1 January 2027–31 December 2028. Base amortisation: 60 quarterly equal-principal payments, 30 September 2028–30 June 2043, with no operating balloon. Under delay, repayments start at the first quarter-end after actual COD and amortise the actual COD balance across the remaining dates to **unchanged final maturity**. No assumed automatic waiver or maturity extension.

Floating benchmark 2.25% plus 225 bp margin; zero benchmark floor; ACT/365. These are hypothetical rates, not current quotes. Construction debt is fully floating. At actual COD, assume an amortising hedge on 70% of scheduled balances to fixed maturity at a 2.25% benchmark strike; the remaining 30% floats. Base interest is therefore 4.5% before fees. Interest-rate stresses change exposed floating rates, not the fixed leg or margin. Hedge execution and closeout risk remain disclosed limits.

## Waterfall, reserves and covenants

Cash is applied to operating costs and tax, scheduled net interest/principal, reserve replenishment and only then permitted distributions or subordinated repayments. Maintenance capex is already deducted in CFADS and must not be deducted twice. DSRA drawings, releases and sponsor injections never increase CFADS.

The DSRA target equals the next **two quarters** of scheduled principal plus net interest. S$6m is a planning envelope, not the calculated target. Phase 4 must identify any shortfall and apply explicit support caps.

| Illustrative term | Proposed test / consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum DSCR | 1.20x each service quarter; breach blocks distributions and enters a 30-day remedial process. Payment failures are separately flagged immediately. |
| Distribution condition | Trailing and forward 12-month minimum quarterly DSCR both at least 1.30x; use available history in year one. Require at least one completed service quarter, fully funded reserves and no continuing breach. |
| Structural leverage | Senior commitment / defined permanent funded uses at most 70%; no additional debt without consent. |
| Completion | Independent engineer signoff, interconnection, valid rights/PPAs, funded costs and DSRA; latest COD 31 December 2028. |
| Interest hedge | 70% of scheduled balances through fixed maturity, subject to actual execution. |
| Sponsor facilities | S$53m permanent equity; separate caps of S$15m GST bridge, S$20m completion support and S$10m cash cures. All hypothetical. |
| Cash cures | Never restate organic DSCR or add injections to CFADS. Cash can remedy a payment/reserve deficit; a historical ratio breach still needs a documented waiver or agreed cure mechanism, such as debt reduction and a forward retest. |
| Security | Assumed share pledge, SPV asset/account security, assignments of PPAs/site rights/insurance and direct agreements with step-in rights. No enforceability opinion is claimed. |
| Reporting | Monthly construction cost/progress reports; quarterly financial and covenant reports; annual audited SPV accounts. |

Real-world conditions would require rooftop agreements, structural/grid studies, site/unit AC licensing analysis [S21], resource assessment, executed EPC/O&M/PPAs, offtaker reviews and insurance/tax/legal diligence. Those conditions are assumed where needed for the simulation, not represented as fulfilled.

**Not yet calculated:** project DSCR, value, sustainable loan size, covenant headroom or a credit recommendation. Test the original structure first; quantify any deficiencies openly before considering revisions.
