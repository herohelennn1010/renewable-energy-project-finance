# Case definition — modelling brief v1

**Information cutoff: 17 November 2026, Singapore time.** Independent educational simulation, with no affiliation with DBS or Sembcorp and no actual financing request.

## Borrower and asset

Borrower: **Project B Solar Portfolio SPV (hypothetical)**, assumed indirectly wholly owned by Sembcorp Industries. This is a descriptive placeholder, not an incorporated company. DBS is the hypothetical lender and decision-maker; neither company has agreed to these terms.

Asset: **120 MWp DC / 100 MWac** of new commercial and industrial rooftop solar in Singapore, illustratively 60 sites of 2 MWp each. This is not Tengeh, Kranji, Pandan, RTX or another announced Sembcorp project. No actual roofs, customer identities, licences, offtaker ratings or signed contracts are invented.

Aggregation is a chosen simulation assumption, informed by Singapore's rooftop deployment context [S12,S13]. It does not establish site availability. At 120 MWp, the hypothetical project equals approximately 5.13% of June 2026 national installed solar capacity. It is therefore a material multi-site programme; that comparison is context, not a market-share forecast.

## Revenue and timeline

Assume 80% of metered production is sold under 20-year, fixed-price, as-generated PPAs; 20% is sold through a licensed settlement counterparty. The PPA price includes environmental attributes. No firm-volume replacement power obligation is assumed. After PPA expiry, all production becomes merchant. Counterparties remain unnamed and unassessed; no sovereign or Sembcorp offtake guarantee is presumed.

Financial close: 1 January 2027. Construction: January 2027–June 2028. Base COD: 1 July 2028. Operation: 25 years through 30 June 2053, with assumed site rights through 30 June 2054. All sites commission together in the simplified baseline; early-site revenue is excluded.

Delays shift generation and actual-COD-linked PPA start, but do not automatically extend the fixed loan maturity, roof rights or 31 December 2028 construction longstop. Beyond longstop, PPA continuation and undrawn senior funding require explicit waivers. The model must flag these conditions rather than assume them away.

## Funding and recourse

Requested senior commitment: **S$100m**. Planning permanent funding envelope: **S$153m**, including **S$53m sponsor equity**. These are budget amounts, not evidence of cash-flow debt capacity. Actual debt drawings can be below commitment.

Separately assume capped sponsor facilities: S$15m for recoverable GST timing, S$20m for construction overruns/completion and S$10m for limited cash cures. None is an actual or verified Sembcorp commitment. Their availability must be explicit, and above-cap deficits remain visible. No full post-completion parent guarantee is assumed.

## Accounting and model perimeter

SGD nominal cash flows; no project FX exposure. Construction is monthly; operation and debt service are quarterly, with annual summaries. Roof access payments enter cash opex; a project IFRS 16 lease-accounting schedule is outside scope. Sponsor historical leases remain separately visible.

Book depreciation and tax allowances are separate. The current headline corporate tax rate of 17% and GST rate of 9% are verified [S17,S20]. Holding them constant, assuming equipment eligibility and assuming full GST recovery are hypothetical choices. Recoverable GST is excluded from permanent costs but separately timed and financed.

No grants, subsidies, temporary tax rebates, group relief, terminal sale proceeds or separate REC income support the base case. Public consolidated sponsor accounts do not establish parent distributable cash, project non-recourse capacity or legally available sponsor support. All material gaps are retained in the decision log.
