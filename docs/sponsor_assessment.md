## Profitability and cash generation

FY2025 revenue changed by -9.6% against FY2024; management EBITDA margin moved from 27.0% to 26.2%. Reported owner profit and non-GAAP underlying earnings should not be interchanged. The issuer attributes changes across segments to different operating conditions; its FY2025 results release discusses weaker gas earnings and contributions from renewables/urban operations [S06]. That explanation is issuer commentary, not an independent causal estimate.

OCF declined 17.1% in FY2025. OCF less cash capex was S$208m before financing interest. Including dividends received, then deducting cash interest and lease principal, leaves a narrower S$55m proxy before the excluded investments/distributions. Company-reported FCF is not used as a substitute for this definition. These differences matter when judging incremental equity support.

## Leverage, liquidity and refinancing

Net debt / management EBITDA rose from 3.63x in FY2023 to 5.16x in FY2025. Gross borrowings exclude leases; the separate lease-inclusive net-debt sensitivity is S$8,594m. Associated/JV earnings can help group profitability while their cash distributions differ from accounting profits. Consequently adjusted EBITDA leverage and coverage deserve a cash-flow qualification.

At December 2025, borrowings due within one year were S$1,057m; maturities in years one to five were S$4,100m and beyond five years S$3,799m. Cash-flow-statement cash was S$1,091m, compared with S$1,109m balance-sheet cash. The difference is pledged/restricted cash in the cash-flow reconciliation [S01].

The S$2,495m undrawn committed facility figure is dated December 2025 [S01, pp14–15]. It cannot be carried unchanged into the post-Alinta position. Uncommitted facilities and debt-programme limits are not equivalent to immediately drawable committed cash. Neither consolidated cash nor facility headroom proves funds are legally available to the parent or this SPV.

The standalone parent's disclosed cash was S$101m in June 2026 versus S$164m at December 2025 [S03, Company columns]. Its intra-group financial guarantees covered S$16,035m of facilities, with S$8,779m drawn [S03, p15]. These overlap group financing and must not be added mechanically to consolidated debt. Standalone cash is a relevant constraint, but it is not an available-support certificate: other obligations, upstream distributions and facility conditions still need diligence.

## Commitments and subsequent events

June 2026 maturity buckets are S$1,420m within one year, S$7,364m in years one to five and S$6,406m thereafter. Disclosed external financial guarantees are S$137m after release of the former SEIL guarantees. They should not be added again to consolidated borrowings without considering scope. Parent intra-group guarantees are disclosed separately and can overlap consolidated obligations. Unprovided commitments total S$1,290m, including S$1,164m for contracted PPE [S03, pp14–15,29].

The 28 October COD of a 600 MW plant is evidence of an operational milestone [S22], not a basis to invent post-June profit or deleveraging. Official October gas-disruption remarks describe supply mitigation at system level [S23]; company-specific earnings impact is not quantified here. The interim issuer release also identifies China curtailment/resource/tariff pressures [S07]. Treat these as risks and issuer explanations rather than assigned loss estimates.

## Implication for the hypothetical financing

The sponsor has operating scale and disclosed earnings/cash generation, but its post-acquisition leverage and competing commitments make reliance on an unspecified parent backstop inappropriate. S$53m of proposed permanent equity is a simulation funding requirement, not an actual demonstrated Sembcorp commitment. Additional capped support is also fictional and must be tested explicitly.

Require project CFADS, realistic offtaker/site diligence and legally documented support before a real lending conclusion. In this case study, proceed to modelling under transparent assumptions; do not infer approval or rejection from sponsor reputation, a single ratio or the balanced budget.
