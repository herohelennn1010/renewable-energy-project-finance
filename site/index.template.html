PATH TO RECONSIDERATION

Fund lifecycle reserves, agree explicit covenant treatment, re-size debt against the revised downside, and verify site rights, offtake and sponsor support. A revised approvable structure has not been modelled.

05 / Evidence & boundaries

An auditable case study.

Information cutoff: 17 November 2026. Model forecasts and lending terms are hypothetical. Public sponsor observations are separately sourced.

Inspect the validation reports ↗
What has been checked?

55 model checks, 44 source-and-budget consistency checks and 15 release checks passed. Workbook sheets and all five memo pages were visually reviewed. Native Microsoft Excel application recalculation was not independently verified. Consistency checks are not independent assurance of the sources.

Which inputs are hypothetical?

The 120 MWp portfolio, 60 sites, PPAs, yield, costs, financing terms and sponsor support are assumptions. No actual project contracts, site rights or financing commitments are asserted. See the assumption register for classifications and rationales.

Open the assumption register ↗
Why reject if the base case pays its debt?

Retained cash provides payment liquidity, but does not change organic CFADS or the replacement-quarter covenant breach. The downside also has temporary payment arrears. The severe case has a construction gap, so its operating results cannot establish viability.
